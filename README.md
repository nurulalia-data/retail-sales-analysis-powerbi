# Retail Sales Analysis – Power BI

## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales and profit performance across a chain of retail stores using Power BI. The analysis explores product, customers, regional, discounts, and return performance to identify key business trends and provide actionable recommendations.

The goal is to identify key sales and profit trends, assess return performance, and support data-driven business decision-making.

## Business Questions

- How are sales and profit performing across product categories?
- Which products and subcategories contribute most to sales and profit?
- How do discounts affect profitability?
- Which product categories and subcategories have higher return rates?
- Which customers and regions show strong or weak performance?
- What actions can be taken to improve business performance?

## Tools Used

- Google Sheets
- Power Query
- Power BI
- DAX

## Dashboard Pages

1. *Overall Performance* – Overview of sales, profit, orders, and key performance indicators.
2. *Sales & Profit Analysis* – Analysis of sales, profit, discounts, product subcategories, and shipping modes.
3. *Returns & Risk Analysis* – Analysis of order return rates by year, product category, and product subcategory.
4. *Customer & Product Insights* – Analysis of product profitability, top-selling products, customers, and geographic sales performance.
5. *Key Recommendations* – Summary of key findings and recommended business actions.

## Key Insights

- Order return rates increased over time, with a notable increase in 2025.
- Technology recorded the highest order return rate among the product categories.
- Machines had the highest order return rate among product subcategories.
- High discounts were associated with weaker profitability in some subcategories.
- Some high-sales customers generated relatively low or negative profit.

## Recommendations

- Investigate high-return products and subcategories, particularly Machines.
- Review discount strategies for products with weak or negative profitability.
- Review pricing and discount levels for low-profit customers.
- Maintain strong performance in high-performing regions and customer segments.
